How this model was built

Floor plan = layout and dimensions. Photos = appearance and object identity. Original mall simulator = simulation architecture.

Coordinate system

1 world unit = 1 metre. Origin at the inside north-west corner of the hall at floor level. +X = east (building length), +Z = south (building width), +Y = up. Heading θ is measured from +X toward +Z. The 3D geometry, the 5 cm collision grid, the 10 cm planning grid and the sensor ray-casts all use this frame.

Inconsistencies and how they were resolved

    What is not real hardware

    • The AMR is the team's proposed design (1.50 × 1.25 × 0.40 ft chassis, two-wheel differential drive, four casters, removable pallet on four corner rods, centre-top LiDAR, depth camera behind the front-panel slot). It has not been built.
    • LiDAR scans, depth points, battery, payload, speeds and wheel speeds are all simulated from this model.
    • The future drone is not modelled. Only its reserved floor area is shown.
